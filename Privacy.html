Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

ConnectUp is a social app where people share posts, photos, videos and stories, follow each other, join groups and send messages. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. By creating an account you agree to this policy.

1. Who runs ConnectUp

ConnectUp is run by [YOUR FULL NAME] (“we”, “us”).
Contact: [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS]

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored only in a protected form by our login provider, never visible to us), full name and username.
  • Profile details: the profile photo and bio you choose to add.
  • Content you create: posts, photos, videos you upload, video links, comments, stories, group posts and the groups you create or join.
  • Messages: the private messages you send and receive.
  • Purchases: whether you hold a paid badge and until when.
  • Activity: who you follow and who follows you, likes, alerts, people you block and reports you send.
  • Settings on your device: your theme and accent colour, and your username, are saved in your browser’s local storage.
  • Usage and device information: the pages and features you use, your approximate location (such as country or city, worked out from your IP address), your device and browser type, and identifiers (such as cookies) set by our analytics and advertising tools.

We do not ask for your phone number, exact location, contacts or payment details.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and log you in.
  • To show your profile, posts and stories to other people and to deliver your messages and alerts.
  • To suggest people through search and to let you follow, block and report.
  • To review reports, remove content that breaks our rules and restrict accounts that abuse the app.
  • To keep the app working and safe.
  • To understand how the app is used so we can fix problems and improve it (analytics).
  • To show advertisements and measure how well they work, which helps pay for the app.
  • To confirm purchases, such as the verified badge, and to switch the paid feature on for your account.

4. What other people can see

  • Visible to other signed-in users: your name, username, profile photo, bio, posts, comments, stories, followers and following, and your group activity. Your email address is also saved in your profile record, which other signed-in users’ apps can technically read, even though the app does not display it.
  • Private messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. They are not end-to-end encrypted, so the service provider and the app operator could technically access them in the database.
  • Do not share anything you would not want others to see.

5. Services we use

We rely on trusted providers who process data for us:

  • Google Firebase (login and database): stores your account and all app data.
  • Cloudinary: stores and delivers videos you upload.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app’s pages and may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit.
  • YouTube and Vimeo: when a post contains a video link from these services, your browser loads their player, and they may collect data under their own policies.
  • Paystack: processes payments for paid features such as the verified badge. It handles your card or bank details itself. We only receive confirmation of payment and the username you enter on the payment form.
  • Google Analytics: collects usage statistics about how people use the app.
  • Advertising partners, such as Google AdSense or a similar ad network: show ads in the app and may collect data to do so.

These providers may process data on servers in other countries. We do not sell your personal information to anyone. Analytics and advertising partners receive only the information described in section 6.

6. Cookies, analytics and advertising

  • Essential storage: the app saves small items in your browser, such as your login session, theme, accent colour and username, to make the app work.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which features people use. It uses cookies or similar identifiers and collects information such as your device, browser, approximate location and the pages you view. You can opt out with Google’s tool at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Advertising: the app shows, or will show, advertisements provided by partners such as Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, including ads based on your interests where the law allows, and to measure results. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.
  • Your consent: where the law requires it, we ask for your permission before analytics and advertising cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time.

Ads are labelled as ads. Advertisers do not get your name, email address or messages from us.

7. How long we keep information

  • Your information stays until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Stories are hidden from other people after 24 hours but may remain stored until you delete them.
  • When you delete your account in Profile, we remove your profile, username, posts, follows and alerts, and your login. Messages you sent stay in the conversations of the people you sent them to. Comments you made on other people’s posts and videos you uploaded may remain until we remove them, so contact us if you want them erased.
  • We may keep reports and records of bans for as long as needed to keep the community safe.

8. Security

Login and data storage are protected by Firebase security and access rules. No service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name, bio, photo and username in Profile.
  • Delete your posts, comments and stories at any time.
  • Block people so you no longer see their content.
  • Delete your account in Profile.
  • Withdraw your consent to analytics and advertising cookies, or opt out of them, using the links in section 6 or your browser settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to have it erased. Contact us at the email above and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

ConnectUp is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by law where you live). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 for analytics or advertising. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the date at the top, and if the changes are important we will tell you in the app.
